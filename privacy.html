Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This is a personal, non-commercial project. It sends text message alerts from a home security system to a single phone number belonging to the system's owner.

What information is collected

The only phone number used by this system is the owner's own phone number, which the owner has entered themselves to receive alerts. No other personal data is collected, stored, or processed from any third party.

How information is used

The phone number is used solely to deliver security alert text messages and calls to the owner. It is not used for marketing, advertising, or any purpose other than delivering these alerts.

Data sharing

No data is shared, sold, or disclosed to any third party, affiliate, or lead generator at any time.

Opt-out

Since the recipient is the system's owner and sole user, opt-out is controlled directly by the owner disabling the system.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the system owner.
